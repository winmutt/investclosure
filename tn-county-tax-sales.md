# Tennessee County Tax Foreclosure Listings

38 TN mountain counties from our scraper. Each entry shows where and when the county's official website states they publish delinquent tax forfeiture/deed sale information.

## Format Legend

- **Authority** — who handles the sale (Clerk & Master, Trustee, Chancery Court, Auditor)
- **Where Listed** — official website/page for property lists and sale dates
- **Schedule** — published sale frequency/dates
- **Notes** — key details from the official site

## Key Findings

1. **Only 4 out of 60+ TN counties have published tax sale property lists online**:
   - Hamilton (Auditor) — has a dedicated list
   - Knox (Trustee) — annual auction, details published
   - Sevier (Clerk & Master) — has FAQ page, but "no properties available"
   - Nashville/Davidson (Clerk & Master) — monthly auction schedule

2. **Most TN counties advertise tax deeds in newspapers** per Tenn. Code Ann. § 67-5-2501. The tnpublicnotice.com site is the state-authorized venue.

3. **Johnson County has no confirmed tax deed sale date or list** for 2026. Clerk & Master's Chancery Court site only lists judicial foreclosure auctions, not tax deeds.

4. **35 of 38 counties in our TN list** have no official web presence for tax deed sales. Their trustee pages on `tennesseetrustee.org` are just property tax search tools.

5. **Tennessee is a "Redeemable Deed" state** — buyers get a deed but the former owner has 1–3 years to redeem at 12%/year interest.
