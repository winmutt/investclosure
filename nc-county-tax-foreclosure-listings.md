## NC Tax Foreclosure Process (Quick Reference)

All NC counties follow the same state statute (N.C.G.S. § 105-374):

1. Taxes delinquent on **January 6** following September 1 due date
2. County may initiate **judicial foreclosure** or **in rem** proceedings
3. After sale, **10-day upset bid period** begins
4. Anyone can submit **upset bid** (minimum 5% or $750, whichever greater)
5. Each accepted upset bid **restarts the 10-day clock**
6. Sale final after **10 days with no upset bids**
7. **Court confirmation** required before sale is complete
